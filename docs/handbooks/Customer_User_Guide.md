# Customer User Guide — FinTrack Pro

Welcome to FinTrack Pro! This guide will help you navigate your finance dashboard, interact with the AI assistant, manage records, and export reports.

---

## 1. Dashboard & Financial Ledger Management
- **Executive Summary Dashboard**: View real-time revenue, expense breakdowns, profit margins, and valuation charts.
- **Adding Transactions**: Navigate to **Finance Records** -> **New Record**. Enter amount, category, date, and optional receipt description.

---

## 2. AI Financial Assistant Chat
- Click the **AI Assistant** icon in the bottom right corner or navigation bar.
- Type prompts in natural language, e.g. "What was our highest expense category last month?" or "Generate a summary for our Q3 board meeting."
- Click **Export to PowerPoint** or **Generate PowerBI Report** directly from the AI response actions.
